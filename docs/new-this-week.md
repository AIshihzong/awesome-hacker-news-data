## 2026-11-03: first review of the candidates queue

26 projects added after reading each source, and one dataset. The list now has 76 projects and 9 datasets.

- **New kinds of "HackerTube for X"**: [hackerrepos](https://jatingargiitk.github.io/hackerrepos/) ranks 29,109 GitHub repos, [DesignerTube](https://www.designertube.app) picks 1,153 videos for designers, [PsychTube](https://github.com/contofalskyR/PsychTube) does psychology, and a [second HackerBooks](https://hacker-books-ecru.vercel.app) ranks 1,800 books from the Algolia API.
- **HackerTube on every screen**: Samsung, LG and Fire TV apps, native Android and iOS apps, and two Chrome extensions that put HN's picks into YouTube itself.
- **Rebuilds that change the rule**: [markokovac16/hackertube](https://github.com/markokovac16/hackertube) needs 8+ people across 3+ years, and [HacKiroTube](https://github.com/sanjayvs-shellkode/HacKiroTube) hunts for re-uploads of deleted videos.
- **Three more Chinese HackerBooks editions**, and [Offbranch](https://thesybuilder.github.io/offbranch/), a topic map of the videos in English and Chinese.
- **A new dataset**: [OpenPipe/hacker-news](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenPipe/hacker-news), used by [cheap-hackertube](https://github.com/saqibns/cheap-hackertube) to test whether a plain frequency count can stand in for HackerTube's LLM filtering.
- The `HackerTube data` label now also covers apps that read HackerTube's public JSON API.

Not added: data mirrors, personal copies, empty repos, and projects that borrow the name but not HN data.

## Week of 2026-11-03: first edition

The list launched with 50 projects and 8 datasets, each labelled with the data it actually uses.
Highlights from the first week of the HackerTube wave:

- [HackerTube](https://hackertube.org) started it on 2026-10-31 and reached [2,841 points](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50319510) on HN. `open-index`
- [HackerBooks](https://hackerbooks.org) followed a day later with ~16.5k books and [1,829 points](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50326821). `not stated`
- [Wikihack](https://wikihack.netlify.app) did the same for Wikipedia articles, at [338 points](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50333339). `not stated`
- HackerTube moved to the TV: [Jnaina/hackertube-tvos](https://github.com/Jnaina/hackertube-tvos) for Apple TV and [hackertube.tv](https://github.com/ephraimduncan/hackertube.tv) for Android TV.
- [HackerHype](https://epestr.com/hackerhype/) now indexes the Hacker* sites themselves, and an [Ask HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50336496) asked whether anyone is worried about the flood.

Also new in the repo: [projects grouped by data source](by-source.md), and a
[Show HN section](../README.md#is-show-hn-still-worth-it-the-evidence) for anyone about to post their own.
